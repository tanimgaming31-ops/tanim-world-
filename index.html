My Poems

Poem 1

LIFE

I feel so incredibly alone today. Even though I have come this far in life, I feel like I am slowly losing myself somewhere along the way.

Even though my family is around me, it still feels like I have no one of my own. I cannot show anyone the intense pain I go through at night. I have never let anyone truly understand the depth of the tears I have cried.

This deep emotional pain no longer allows spring to enter my life. Even my friends seem to be slipping away as I become more and more isolated.

Sometimes I feel like I would have given up long ago... but the thought that it would be a sin is one of the reasons I am still holding on and taking each breath.

— TaNiM

Poem 2

LIFE (2)

What hurts me the most is thinking about myself. I gave importance to the wrong person and ended up neglecting myself.

I shared my thoughts, my dreams, and the deepest parts of my heart with someone I trusted, only to realize that I had trusted the wrong person.

I got caught up in someone's fake affection and lost so much precious time in my life. Now, whenever I look back and remember everything, the person I feel the most sorry for is myself.

It hurts deeply when I think about how I allowed myself to go through all of this. Maybe my fault was greater than the person who was pretending, because I failed to understand the difference between acting and genuine feelings.

Now I'm paying the price for that mistake myself. I just hope that in this lifetime, I learn how to recognize people better, understand the difference between truth and lies, and never forget my own worth again. 😅💔

— TaNiM

Poem 3

LIFE: Friendship Means…

Friendship is not about being close; it is about always being there for each other.

It is not about comforting someone with lies, but about giving them courage with the truth.

It is not about walking away when someone makes a mistake, but about helping them correct it with love.

It is not about changing with time, but about standing together through every season of life.

It is not just about asking, “How are you?” It is about understanding how someone feels, even when they cannot find the words.

It is not just about putting an arm around someone’s shoulder for a photo, but about placing a comforting hand on their shoulder during their darkest and loneliest moments.

In today’s busy and fast-paced life, friendship means not letting distance, time, or responsibilities weaken the bond.

Friendship is not built on selfishness, but on trust. Not on convenience, but on loyalty. Not only in happy moments, but in every joy, every struggle, and every chapter of life.

That is the true meaning of friendship. ❤️

— TaNiM

Poem 4

LIFE: Trust

Do you know what the most valuable thing in the world is?

It is trust.

Not money. Not gold. But the trust of a human heart.

If you lose money, you can earn it again. If you lose gold, you can replace it.

But once trust is broken, you may be forgiven, yet things are never quite the same again.

Because forgiveness can heal the pain, but it cannot always restore the place that trust once held.

— TaNiM
